✨ AI notes

When a song isn't in the library, Pianiol asks Claude to write out the melody and accompaniment from its knowledge of the piece, then plays it on your instrument. Calls go straight from this device to api.anthropic.com using your own key.

Get a key at console.anthropic.com → API keys, and set a spending limit there. A typical song costs a few cents. Declined requests fall back to another Claude model automatically.

Listen mode

Play the song out loud — from the video, another device, or your own instrument. Pianiol listens and writes down the melody. Works best with a single clear melody line.
